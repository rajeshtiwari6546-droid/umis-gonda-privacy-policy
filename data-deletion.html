Account & Data Deletion Request

UMIS Gonda School Management App

You can request deletion of your UMIS app account and associated personal data by contacting the school Principal through WhatsApp.

Before you request deletion: Please make sure you are the account holder, parent/guardian, or an authorised representative. The school may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the WhatsApp button below.
  2. Send the pre-filled message with the account holder's name and registered phone number. Add the class/role if applicable.
  3. The school will review your request and may contact you to verify it.
Request deletion on WhatsApp

What may be deleted?

After identity verification and subject to applicable legal, school-record, and safety requirements, the school can review deletion of the app account and associated personal data. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate school recordkeeping obligations.

Need help?

Contact the school Principal through the WhatsApp button above and clearly state that your message is an account/data deletion request.

Please do not include passwords, OTPs, or other secret authentication information in your request.
