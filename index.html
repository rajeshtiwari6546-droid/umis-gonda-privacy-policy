Privacy Policy - UMIS Gonda

School: URMILA MADHAV INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

App: UMIS Gonda

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. Introduction

UMIS Gonda is a school management application intended to support school-related activities for students, parents or guardians, teachers, administrators and authorised school staff.

2. Information handled by the app

Depending on the features used and the information entered by the school, the app may handle student and guardian details, school login credentials, attendance, class information, homework, marks, report cards, fee records, announcements and school transport information.

Information actually collected depends on the app's configuration and the features enabled by the school.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate authorised users, provide school management services, display academic and attendance records, communicate school announcements, manage fee records and support school transport functions.

4. Data storage and service providers

The application uses Supabase as part of its backend infrastructure. The data stored or processed there depends on the app's implementation and school configuration. Please contact the school for details about the specific records maintained for your account.

5. Sharing and disclosure

School information should be accessible only to authorised users according to their roles and permissions. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to operate services used by the school. The school should verify the actual access controls and any third-party data sharing before relying on this policy.

6. Children's information

This app may be used in connection with school students, including children. Student information should be provided and managed by the school and authorised parents or guardians as applicable. The school should ensure that appropriate permissions and safeguards are in place.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect school information. Data retention and deletion depend on school requirements, applicable law and the systems in which the information is stored. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User requests and contact

For questions about personal information, correction or deletion requests, please contact URMILA MADHAV INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL through its official communication channels.

School contact details: The school should publish its verified official contact details here before this policy is used.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available at this webpage.
